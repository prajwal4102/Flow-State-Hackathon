Why AI video tools are winning on LinkedIn in 2026

Published by GrowthLoop Media, October 2026

Short explainer videos now out-perform static posts on LinkedIn by roughly three to one in reach. The brands that win post twice a week, keep videos under 45 seconds, open with a number, and close with a single question to drive comments.

Three patterns stand out this quarter:

  • Data-first hooks: a real chart in the first three seconds.
  • Map animations for anything involving logistics, travel or expansion.
  • Captions that state the cost or time saved in the first line.

Diffusion-based video generators still struggle with on-screen text, which is why code-rendered motion graphics are showing up in more B2B feeds.
